# Curated Trading Library

The starter manifest is `data/trading-library.json`. It contains historical market/speculation books available from Project Gutenberg, including:

- The Theory of Stock Exchange Speculation — Arthur Crump
- Successful Stock Speculation — John James Butler
- The Psychology of Speculation — Henry Howard Harper
- Fifty Years in Wall Street — Henry Clews
- The Stock Exchange from Within — William C. Van Antwerp
- The Cycles of Speculation — Thomas Gibson

These are useful mainly for market history, behavioral/psychological patterns, risk discipline, exchange mechanics and recurring speculative behavior. They are **not** a substitute for modern microstructure data, current exchange rules, empirical validation, or backtesting.

Project Gutenberg identifies these editions as public domain in the USA. Rights can differ by jurisdiction, so JARVIS preserves that provenance/license label rather than claiming universal public-domain status.
